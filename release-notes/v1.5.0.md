- New: custom split layouts, like FancyZones. In Split View, the Custom tile opens an editor: click a zone to split it where the line is, Shift turns the line, drag a divider to resize (it snaps to halves, thirds and quarters; hold Alt to place it freely, double-click to even a row). Up to 8 panes
- Split View: a split keeps the shape it was given. Closing a session leaves its pane empty, a tab from outside goes only into an empty pane, and you swap two sessions by dragging one pane's bar onto the other. The split closes with its last session, or with Separate
- Each pane in a split has its own small bar with the session's name; the tab strip shows the split as one tab
- Tab strip: when the tabs do not all fit, arrows at both ends scroll it, and so does the mouse wheel
- Connection list: right-click a folder, Open all connections opens everything in it and in the folders inside it
- Remote Desktop: sessions opened together no longer show up at the top left of the screen or over the app. They stay hidden until their tab is shown and start a moment apart
- Device detection: Dell OS10 and N-series switches are recognised and get the Dell logo (thanks to dtnstk)
- Terminal: dead keys such as AltGr+Ü for ~ on a Turkish keyboard work as elsewhere in Windows (first press nothing, second ~~); after an accent the next key types its own letter
- SFTP shows live speed and checks SHA-256 only when you ask
- No white flash when the app starts
