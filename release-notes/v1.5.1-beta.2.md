- New: drag a connection from the connection list onto an empty pane of a split, and it opens right there, in that pane. It can be taken out of the split again as usual
- Split View: each pane's bar has its own record button, left of its close button. Click it to start recording that session, click again to stop; it is red while recording. The Session logs button at the top is not shown over a split
- Session logs are kept the way the connection list is: in the log folder, the connection's folders, then a folder named after the connection, with one file per day named after it and the date (Name_17_11_2026.txt). A second log on the same day gets (2), and so on. Logs no longer go into folders named after the address
- Fix: Dell switches were not recognised, or taken for a Cisco. Dell N-series switches are now recognised from the head of their running configuration, and Dell OS10 from its login banner. A guess from configuration lines other makers share (such as switchport access vlan, which Dell N-series prints too) is corrected once the device names itself
- From v1.5.1-beta.1: Split View opens on the split on screen, its layout and empty panes as they are
