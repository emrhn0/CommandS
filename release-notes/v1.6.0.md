- A new icon: a folded-paper lightning bolt, the same on light and dark themes
- CommandS Dark and CommandS Light take the icon's colours: warm, near-black surfaces with a coral accent in the dark theme, and white with a deep red accent in the light one. If you were on the built-in dark or light theme, you are on the new one already
- A short launch screen shows the icon, the name, the version and a bar that fills in about two seconds. It never blocks a click
- With no tab open you get a welcome panel: a greeting, New Connection and Import, and the connections you opened most recently, each one click away
- The greeting follows the time of day. It starts with a plain good morning, good afternoon, good evening or good night, then changes through the day with a few lighter lines
