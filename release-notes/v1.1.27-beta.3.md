- Beta is now the same app at a newer version: installing it replaces the normal CommandS on this computer and keeps your connections, settings and saved passwords
- Opening CommandS after installing the beta opens the beta. When the next normal release is out, the app offers it and moves you on to it
- The app only offers normal releases on its own. A beta is installed by hand, from its download or from Settings > Updates with Beta picked
- If you installed beta 1 or 2, they were a separate "CommandS Beta" app. You can remove that one from Windows Settings > Apps
- The update button is green in both light and dark themes
- Also in this beta: tabs for each session of a split view, splitting the tab you are on, the Separate button, the centred window title, less space under the tabs, and better Cisco detection
