- The tab in use is no longer filled with orange: it is raised a step, and a coloured line along its foot shows its folder's colour, so the colour stays visible on the open tab too
- Folder types: a new folder can be Network (blue), Firewall (orange) or System (green), and takes that colour by itself. Those three colours are kept for them
- Color… on a folder lists the three types by name, then the other colours, and a Custom… button for a colour of your own
