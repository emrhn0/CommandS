- Backups of your connections: one every day, and one before every import, delete or restore. The newest 30 daily and 20 others are kept, with passwords still encrypted. Settings > Connections > Backups lists them and restores one with a click. If your saved connections ever cannot be read, nothing is saved over them, and the app offers to restore a backup
- Search in a terminal: the small magnifier at the top right of a session, or Ctrl+Shift+F. Every match is marked, with its number and the total; Enter and the arrows move between them, Esc closes it
- Type into several sessions at once: in Split View, tick the panes on their bars. What you type into one ticked pane goes to all of them; untick to stop
- Credential profiles: one user name and password for many connections, so a changed password is changed once. Make them in Settings > Connections or straight from a connection's Credentials; existing connections are unchanged until you pick a profile
- Favorites: star a connection from its menu. Favorites have a small star in the list and their own place on the welcome panel
- Quick search: the magnifier in the title bar, or Ctrl+K (Ctrl+Shift+K inside a terminal, where Ctrl+K stays the session's). Find any connection or folder by name or address; Enter connects, or shows the folder in the list
- When a device's SSH key has changed (it was replaced, reset or reinstalled), a bar now says so in plain words and can forget the old key and connect again, instead of the long warning from OpenSSH
