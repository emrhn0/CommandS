- New: drag a connection from the connection list onto an empty pane of a split, and it opens right there, in that pane. It can be taken out of the split again as usual
- Split View: each pane's bar has its own record button, left of its close button. Click it to start recording that session, click again to stop; it is red while recording. The Session logs button at the top is not shown over a split
- Session logs are kept the way the connection list is: in the log folder, the connection's folders, then a folder named after the connection, with one file per day named after it and the date (Name_17_11_2026.txt). A second log on the same day gets (2), and so on
- Fix: Dell switches were not recognised, or taken for a Cisco. Dell N-series switches are recognised from the head of their running configuration, and Dell OS10 from its login banner. A guess from configuration lines other makers share is corrected once the device names itself
- Fix: opening Split View over a split showed a built-in layout picked by the number of sessions instead of the split on screen. It now shows that split as it is, its dividers and empty panes included, with its layout marked, built-in or custom
