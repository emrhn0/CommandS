- New (beta): record an SSH session to a file. The record button sits next to Split View; a red dot shows while it runs. Only what the device prints is saved, never what you type
- Logs are plain text: colours and other terminal codes are left out
- Pick the log folder in Settings. By default logs stay in this computer's local app folder, not in Documents, which is often synced to a cloud drive
- A connection can start recording on its own: tick "Record session automatically" in its settings
- New (beta): a button in the title bar hides and shows the connection list. It remembers your choice after a restart
- New (beta): a thin scrollbar appears when the mouse reaches the right edge of a terminal
- From v1.1.32-beta.1: MCP show commands with filters such as "| include A|B|C" run without asking
