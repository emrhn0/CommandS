- Right-click one of several selected folders: the menu now acts on all of them. Color gives them all the same colour or type, Sort contents sorts each, and Delete selected removes the selection
- Power saver has three choices in Settings > General: Off, On, and Same as system. Same as system (the default) follows Windows' Energy Saver or a Mac's Low Power Mode, so plugging in or turning the energy saver off brings the welcome screen's motion back by itself. A line under the choices says what is in effect now
