- Folder types: a new folder can be Network (blue), Firewall (orange) or System (green). It takes that colour by itself and is named after its type until you type a name of your own; those three colours are kept for them
- Color… on a folder lists the three types by name, then "No color", the other colours and a Custom… button for a colour of your own. A choice is applied with Save
- Select several folders and right-click one of them: Color, Sort contents and Delete selected act on all of them
- Several selected connections or folders can be dragged together
- Sort contents on a folder opens its options in a submenu beside it, and Select is now at the top of the list's view menu
- The tab in use is raised a step instead of filled with orange, and a line in its folder's colour runs along its foot, so the colour stays visible on the open tab too
- The title bar's search box turns into the search field right where it is, at the same size, with the results under it. Ctrl+K opens the search where the mouse is
- Much less CPU and graphics power: a minimized window no longer draws at all, and the welcome screen's animations stop while another window is in front
- Power saver in Settings > General: Off, On, or Same as system (the default), which follows Windows' Energy Saver or a Mac's Low Power Mode the moment it changes. While it is in effect, the welcome screen's blinking cursor and pulsing dot stay still
- Record session: the whole "Include history" line takes the click, and its tick box no longer shows a round halo
