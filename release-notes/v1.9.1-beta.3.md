- A home button at the start of the tab bar brings back the welcome screen while your sessions keep running. The open sessions are listed there; a click goes back to one
- The welcome screen counts your connections, open sessions, sessions opened today and folders in small cards, and shows the most useful shortcuts at the bottom
- Before anything is saved, the welcome screen lists the first steps: add a connection, import, see the shortcuts
- Folders can have a colour (a folder's menu, Color…). Folders inside it take the same colour, and so do the tab icons of its connections
- The SSH / RDP filters above the list are plain words, the one in use underlined
- A thin status bar under the session in use shows its protocol, its address (click to copy) and how long it has been open
- In the command palette, type @ and a name or address to open a saved connection
