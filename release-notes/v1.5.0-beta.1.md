- New: custom split layouts, like FancyZones. In Split View, the Custom tile opens an editor: click a zone to split it side by side, Shift+click to stack it, drag a divider to resize, and give it a name. Up to 8 panes. Your layouts sit next to the built-in ones; the pencil on a tile edits or deletes it
- A layout saved with Save as layout keeps a custom shape too, and still opens if that custom layout is deleted later
