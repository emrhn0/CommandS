- Split View: closing a session, or taking it out of the split, now leaves its pane empty instead of letting the panes around it move over. A layout keeps the shape it was given; drop a tab on the empty pane to fill it. An empty pane no longer has a close button
- Custom layout editor: while you drag a divider it shows both shares (for example 33% | 67%) and snaps to halves, thirds and quarters; hold Alt to place it freely. Double-click a divider to make its whole row or column even
- Terminal: AltGr+Ü on a Turkish keyboard no longer types an extra ü before ~. The modifiers are now read as they were when the key was pressed, and as a last guard a key that reports its plain letter while AltGr is held types nothing
- From v1.5.0-beta.5 and earlier: empty panes stay in a split; Open all connections for a folder; custom split layouts; SFTP live speed and on-demand SHA-256 check; no white flash at start
